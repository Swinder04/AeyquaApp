What the app handles

Aeyqua is a standalone aquarium journal. When you use it, the app accesses the tank details, photos you select, water-test readings and sessions, care tasks, completion history, preferences and notes you enter. The installed Android app stores the journal in its private on-device SQLite storage. Aeyqua does not require an account, and the journal is not uploaded to an Aeyqua server. The current app does not include advertising, analytics, cloud sync or automatic backup. The core journal is designed for use without an internet connection; a development preview may connect to its development server and is not the installed release.

The app uses the system photo picker to access only photos you choose to add. It prepares selected photos for storage in your journal. Previously imported external photo links are retained as inert text and are not fetched automatically.

Exports and transfers you control

If you choose Export, Aeyqua prepares a JSON file and opens your device's share sheet. The file can include all tanks (including archived tanks), photos, notes, readings, testing sessions, care tasks, completion history and preferences. The JSON is readable and not encrypted. Your chosen destination—another app, service, device, or person—may receive and retain the copy under its own practices. A temporary local export file may remain in the app's cache until a later cleanup; copies you save or send are outside Aeyqua's control. Protect files you share, especially if they contain notes or photos.

If you choose Import through the system document picker, the file you select is read locally to preview and replace the entire journal on this phone only after your confirmation. Import is not a merge and does not delete the source file or other devices' journals. Export a separate copy first if you want a recoverable version. There is no automatic backup or undo for a completed replacement.

Optional support link

If you tap Support Aeyqua, the app opens a PayPal page in your external browser. The app does not send your aquarium journal to PayPal. PayPal and your browser may process information about your visit and any payment according to their own policies. Support is optional, offers no in-app features or other digital benefits, and is not represented as a charitable tax-deductible donation. The actual PayPal flow and recipient must be verified before publication; if it changes, this section must be reviewed.

Retention and deletion

Your journal remains on your phone until you edit/delete records, choose Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall/remove the app's data. Delete all data clears the local journal in Aeyqua; it does not necessarily immediately erase a temporary JSON export in the app's local cache (that is cleaned on the next app launch or export). It does not recall exports you previously saved/shared, delete files held by a recipient, or delete records held separately by PayPal. Android's own device and storage protections apply; Aeyqua does not claim its local database or exports have app-specific encryption.

The Android configuration disables platform cloud backup for this app. There is no automatic Aeyqua backup. Device loss, uninstall, app-data deletion or an import can permanently remove journal entries unless you have manually saved an export. Device-to-device transfer and native backup behavior must be verified against the final Android release and relevant device versions; do not rely on automatic restore.

Changes and questions

If the app's data handling changes, this policy and the Play Data safety answers must be updated before a new release. For privacy questions, contact swinderspam@gmail.com. There is no account to delete. No Aeyqua-hosted journal exists for the publisher to erase remotely; use the in-app deletion action to erase this phone's journal.
